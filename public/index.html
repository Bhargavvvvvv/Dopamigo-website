A macOS companion for student work

A small next step.
A place to come back to.

Dopamigo helps you break an assignment into manageable actions, keep your notes and resources together, and pick up where you left off.

Currently a development prototype for Apple Silicon Macs.

From an assignment to your next action

Make a start

Add an assignment brief and ask for a plan with short, concrete steps. Edit the steps to fit how you want to work.

Keep your place

Save notes, links and your exact next action. When you return, your workspace keeps the context you left behind.

Find your way back

An optional Chrome companion notices configured browser detours during focus sessions. Gemini writes a contextual reminder, with optional spoken audio.

Your assignments, connected

Optional Google Calendar and Drive connection

Connect Google inside the desktop app to discover assignment deadlines in the next two weeks. Dopamigo creates tasks grouped by subject and looks for matching documents in your Assignments folder.

Calendar and Drive access is read-only. Calendar titles, descriptions and dates are sent to Google Gemini to identify assignments. Drive file and folder names are matched locally; the integration does not download document contents.

You can pause syncing or disconnect. Finding a document does not mark an assignment as complete or submitted.
